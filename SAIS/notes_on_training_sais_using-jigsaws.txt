If you mean JIGSAWS Suturing gestures, SAIS already has a dedicated training branch. The training entrypoint is run_experiments.py, not main.sh, which is the inference pipeline.

The JIGSAWS loader expects precomputed features and annotations in this layout:
- `root/JIGSAWS_Suturing/Results/ViT_SelfSupervised_ImageNet_RepsAndLabels.h5`
- `root/JIGSAWS_Suturing/Results/ViT_SelfSupervised_ImageNet_FlowRepsAndLabels.h5`
- `root/SurgicalPaths/JIGSAWS_Suturing_gestures_timestamps.csv`

That is wired in prepare_dataset.py and prepare_dataset.py. I did not find that `SurgicalPaths` CSV in this workspace, so training will not run until it exists at the expected path.

A typical training command is:

```powershell
python -m torch.distributed.launch SAIS/scripts/run_experiments.py `
  -p e:/antonio/SAIS/SAIS `
  -data JIGSAWS_Suturing_Gestures_Classification `
  -d JIGSAWS_Suturing `
  -m ViT `
  -enc ViT_SelfSupervised_ImageNet `
  -t Prototypes `
  -mod RGB-Flow `
  -dim 384 `
  -bs 2 `
  -lr 1e-1 `
  -nc 10 `
  -bc `
  -sa `
  -domains Suturing `
  -ph train val `
  -dt reps `
  -e 50 `
  -f <number_of_unique_subjects>
```

Use `-f` as the number of unique subjects in your JIGSAWS CSV, because the split is leave-one-subject-out by `Subject` in prepare_dataset.py. The training loop in train.py uses the validation phase for checkpointing, so `train val` is enough for training.
